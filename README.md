# DART: Dynasty Auto-Recruiting Tool

**DART builds every program's recruiting class and transfer portal in one run, so your
whole dynasty restocks itself each offseason.**

You run it once at offseason recruiting week 1. DART works out every team's high school
and JUCO class and places them on the game's own recruiting board, so the game signs
them natively. Then it runs the transfer portal across the whole country and puts the
transfers straight onto rosters. Sim to national signing day and you're done. Every
program comes out of the offseason with a full, believable roster.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **DART only works with its mod installed.** Install `DART.fbmod` (or
> `DART-NoCoachPerks.fbmod`) from the download's `mods` folder before you play the season
> you'll run DART on. The mod blocks the game's own transfer portal and turns off weekly
> recruiting, so the game doesn't fight DART's classes and transfers, and it gives every
> player five seasons. Without it, the game's portal and recruiting run on top of DART's
> and your rosters won't come out right.

## How it works

DART walks you through it step by step:

1. **Pick your save.** DART always writes a new save and never touches your original.
2. **Program standings.** See where every program stands, and raise or cap the ones you
   want.
3. **Fifth years.** Decide who stays for one more season.
4. **Team schemes.** Recruit to what each staff runs.
5. **High school recruiting.** Every program's class, built and signed through the game's
   board. You can assign a specific recruit to a specific program.
6. **Portal odds.** Each player's chance of entering the portal. Keep a player home or push
   one in.
7. **Players entering.** Who's in the portal this year. Redraw it if you want a different
   portal.
8. **Portal results.** Where everyone landed, and why. Send any transfer to a different
   program and the rest of the portal adjusts around him.
9. **Summary.** Review everything, then write the new save.

Click any player to see why he moved, his past teams and his stats. The app updates itself
when a new version is out.

## Install

1. Download the latest release from
   [DART-Releases](https://github.com/KevinMilesCode/DART-Releases/releases/latest) and
   unzip it.
2. **Install one of the two included mods (required):**
   * `DART.fbmod`: sets the redshirt limit to 50 games (every player gets five seasons),
     blocks the game's own transfer portal (DART runs it instead) and sets weekly
     recruiting hours to 0 (DART handles recruiting). Nothing else changes.
   * `DART-NoCoachPerks.fbmod`: the same mod with every coach ability turned off.
3. Advance your dynasty to offseason recruiting week 1, save, and run `DART.exe`.

The mod replaces the dynasty tuning file, so it can't be combined with other mods that
change dynasty tuning.

## Compatibility

* **Fang's recruiting tool: compatible, and recommended.** DART is built to run alongside it.
* **DRI+: not compatible.**
* **Playoff customization mod: not compatible yet.** Support is coming soon.
* **Other mods that change dynasty tuning: not compatible.** The DART mod replaces the
  dynasty tuning file.

## How is this different from MyDynasty Auto Recruiting?

First, MyDynasty is great. I've loved using it, and DART exists because of how much I
enjoyed what it does. I'm just a control freak, and I wanted a few specific things for my
own dynasty. If MyDynasty already works for you, keep using it. If you want more say in how
your college football world plays out, DART might be for you.

* **All players show up on your team in the offseason.** Recruits and transfers are on your
  roster before the season starts.
* **Built-in settings to lift up or cap teams.** Keep the long-term landscape looking the
  way you think is realistic.
* **Granular control over individual players.** Force a recruit to commit to a specific
  program, keep a player out of the portal or push him in, or pick where a transfer lands.
