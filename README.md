# DART: Dynasty Auto-Recruiting Tool

Automatic recruiting for EA SPORTS College Football 27 dynasties. In one run, DART
builds every program's high school class and runs the transfer portal for the whole
league.

## How it works

**High school recruiting.** DART decides where every high school and JUCO recruit
goes, based on each program's standing, needs and recruiting pipelines. It writes
those commitments onto the game's recruiting board at offseason recruiting week 1,
and they hold through national signing day. At signing day the game signs them and
they show up on their new teams.

**Transfer portal.** The included mod blocks the game's own portal. DART decides who
enters and where each player lands, and puts every transfer straight onto his new
roster. Transfers are on their new teams as soon as you load the save, so you see
your team as it will really look next season. Every roster ends at 85.

DART never changes your save. It writes a new one next to it.

## Changing what DART decides

Nothing is written until you choose to. Before that you can override what DART
decides:

- **Options:** how programs are rated (standing, tiers, blue bloods, conference
  floors and caps), class sizes and portal volume.
- **High school recruiting:** run it again for a different set of classes.
- **Portal odds:** keep a player home (someone else from his program goes instead)
  or push a player into the portal. Redraw to pick who enters again.
- **Transfer portal:** send a transfer to a different school, then re-run the portal.
  Everyone else lands around your change, and every roster still ends at 85.

Click any recruit or player to see why he ended up where he did.

## Install

1. Download the latest zip from [Releases](../../releases/latest) and unzip it.
2. Install one mod from the `mods` folder with your mod manager: `DART.fbmod`, or
   `DART-NoCoachPerks.fbmod` if you want coach abilities to have no effect.
3. Advance your dynasty to offseason recruiting week 1 and save.
4. Run `DART.exe`, pick the save, follow the steps, and write the new save.
5. Load the new save in the game and sim to national signing day.

DART checks for a new version when it starts and can update itself.
